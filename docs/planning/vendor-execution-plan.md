# Vendor Execution Plan

This plan captures the major delivery priorities for the vendor-side product experience.

## Phase 1 priorities

- go-live safety UX
- menu and table-ordering foundation
- minimum KYC requirements
- payment collection modes
- bill request support from the customer side
- basic staff accounts and simplified role access

## Phase 1.1 additions

- cancelled cheque support for bank verification
- business entity type coverage in payout workflows
- call waiter interaction
- add-on selection constraints
- spice level support
- KOT basic settings

## Later-phase items

- advanced KYC verification lifecycle
- granular role matrix
- advanced printer networking and diagnostics
- deeper compliance automation and tax reporting
- delivery and driver tracking

## Interpretation

This plan is useful as a prioritization lens, not as a promise that every listed item is already fully production-complete. It explains how vendor operations have been staged across release waves.
